# Code of Conduct

This is a learning space. Many people here are making their **first ever** open-source contribution.

## Our standards

- ✅ Be kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to beginners.
- ✅ Give helpful, respectful feedback on code, not on people.
- ✅ Assume good intentions.
- ✅ Ask questions freely. There are no "stupid" questions here.
- ❌ No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- ❌ No spam PRs (e.g. changing one word just to get a PR count).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs or issues, or block users who don't follow these rules. If you see a problem, open an issue or contact a maintainer.

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
